# Chickaree

Chickaree is a mobile app that makes it easy to track your work hours, earnings, and expenses in one place.

It is designed especially for Work and Travel participants, and anyone managing multiple jobs.

[Download on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6762767337)

## Our Goal

Chickaree helps users clearly understand how much they work, how much they earn, and where their money goes.

You can log your work hours, calculate your earnings automatically, and track your financial goals without dealing with complicated spreadsheets or manual calculations.

## Features

- Add and manage multiple jobs
- Set an hourly rate for each job
- Log work hours and tips
- Automatically calculate weekly earnings
- Calculate overtime pay
- View income and expenses in one place
- Organize expenses by category
- Scan receipts with AI
- Set weekly income goals
- View monthly and all-time summaries

## Free Plan

You can start using Chickaree for free.

The free plan allows you to add up to 2 jobs and use the essential work-hour and earnings tracking features.

## Chickaree Pro
